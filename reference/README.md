# Reference material

Visual reference for the rebuild. Not shipped with the game and not loaded at runtime.

## `charlie/` — the star

| File | What it shows | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `IMG_0467.jpeg` | Head-on close-up | **Primary head reference.** Domed skull, short muzzle, low-set ears, big round eyes, white blaze |
| `IMG_8224.jpeg` | Full body, sitting, red heart bandana | **Primary body reference** + the bandana accessory |
| `IMG_0373.jpeg` | Full body outdoors on grass | Proportions, brown saddle patch, coat markings |
| `IMG_2100.JPG` | Wearing turquoise sunglasses | The sunglasses accessory |
| `IMG_6040.jpeg` | Wearing a gold bow tie | The bow tie accessory |
| `IMG_8789.jpeg` | Wearing a red-and-white striped sweater | The sweater accessory |
| `charlie_figurine.png` | Figurine holding a tennis ball | Why he carries a ball in his mouth in-game |

Colours are deliberately **not** sampled literally from these — most are shot in shade, and Crossy Road's palette is flat and saturated. The stylised values derived from them are in SPEC §3.8.

## `cr_*.png` — the original game

App Store gameplay screenshots of *Crossy Road* by Hipster Whale, used to measure the camera and sample the world palette. Theirs, not ours; kept here only as visual reference.

`cr_s031.png` is the camera-angle benchmark: measuring the tilt of the logs and road bands across these shots gives ≈13° down-right, which solves to the 57° pitch / 15° yaw in SPEC §3.1.
